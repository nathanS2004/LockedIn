# Calibrate the focus border to the laptop

The original two-corner placement estimated the distance to the screen. A change in viewing position could expose that depth mismatch, especially across the keyboard.

## Place a border

1. Open **Create Border** or press **B** outside the menu. Use the cyan point a few centimeters in front of the right controller as a 3D marker. Move the controller itself close enough to line up the point with the physical corner.
2. Pull the right **trigger** at the **top-left**, **top-right**, and **bottom-left** of the real screen, in that order.
3. Press **A** for the screen only. To include the keyboard, move the cyan point to the front-center edge of the keyboard and pull the **trigger** instead.
4. Lean forward and sideways. The visible region should continue to cover the same physical area. Press **B** to recalibrate or **A** to bypass the selected effect.

The screen corners establish the actual screen angle, width, and height. The optional point adds depth for the keyboard. Both eyes use their tracked positions when projecting the calibrated volume. The border is fixed in the room for the current session. Moving the laptop requires recalibration.

## Verification

Test both placement modes on Quest. Check that calibration starts with ordinary passthrough and that the blue or green background leaves the screen and optional keyboard visible while leaning forward or sideways.
